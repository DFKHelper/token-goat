Limitations, all of which travel with the numbers above

  - n = 6 clean pairs. A median over six pairs moves under
    single-pair changes; the spread above is the honest picture.
  - Every pair resolved in both arms, so no capability claim follows.
  - 2 pairs excluded post hoc for writer contamination. The criterion
    is mechanical and outcome-blind, but was not pre-registered.
  - Attrition was duration-correlated: the crash destroyed long runs
    preferentially, and 2 tasks were lost entirely.
  - The run was truncated at n=7 with the ratios already visible.
    Task selection stayed blind; the decision to stop did not.
  - Single model, single repository, tasks authored by this project.
